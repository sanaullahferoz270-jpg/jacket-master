About Jacket Masters

Built for
the moment.

Jacket Masters makes modern premium outerwear for the moments that matter — the early flight, the late walk home, the first properly cold morning of the year.

We keep the silhouette simple and put the effort where you feel it: warmth without bulk, shells that shrug off weather, and details built to last well beyond one season.
